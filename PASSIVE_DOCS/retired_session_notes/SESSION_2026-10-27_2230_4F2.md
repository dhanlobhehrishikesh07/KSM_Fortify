## What was asked
Reconcile the three "Online copy" settings (`SYNC_TASKS`, `SYNC_CHAT`, `SYNC_AUDITS_SCANS`): they were marked `restart=True` although the workers already read them live. Make the flag match reality.

## What was done
- Checked the workers: `TaskSync`, `ChatSync` and `RecordSync` read their switch on every call and set `engine.enabled = True` themselves, so turning a switch off stops sending and turning it on resumes sending, even when the engine was built disabled at start-up (all switches off). The flag was wrong; the behaviour was right.
- One real gap found: turning `SYNC_TASKS` on live did nothing until the next task change, so the backup and first upload of existing tasks waited. Fixed with a nudge.
- `settings_store.py`: `restart=True` removed from the three switches; help text now says "Applies at once".
- `web_api.py`: new private `_apply_sync_switches(keys)`, called from `_on_settings_changed`. For each changed sync switch it calls that sync's `sync_now()` (does nothing when the switch is off; errors are logged, never raised). No new public attribute or method on `WebAPI`.
- Result: the Settings tab no longer shows the RESTART badge or the "Restart the app to apply" note for these three.
