## What was asked
Save each strategy audit and screen-scan answer with its question and time to the owner's Firebase, under the "Keep audits and screen scans online" switch. Never the image.

## What was done
- New `app/record_sync.py` (`RecordSync`, `start_record_sync`): `record_audit` writes `ksm/audits/<time_key>` (question, answer, time); `record_scan` writes `ksm/scans/<time_key>` (question, answer, monitor, model, time). Uses `engine.queue_record` (keys removed by the engine) and flushes only the `ksm/audits/` and `ksm/scans/` paths. Same-second records get a `-NN` suffix. Answers cut at 40000 chars, questions at 2000.
- `cloud_sync.py`: added `AUDIT_ROOT` and `SCAN_ROOT` constants only.
- `web_api.py`: new optional `record_sync` argument; `_perform_audit` and `_run_screen_scan` call private hooks after a successful result. Failed runs are not saved. Audit sends a fixed question label, never the task matrix. Scan hook receives text only (not `preview_b64`, sizes or bytes). Hook errors are caught and logged. No new public attribute on `WebAPI`.
- `main_window.py`: `start_online_copy` now returns `(task_sync, chat_sync, record_sync)`; `run()` passes `record_sync=online[2]`.
- `firebase/database.rules.json`: validation for `ksm/audits/$id` and `ksm/scans/$id`.
- `tests/test_chat_sync.py`: two assertions updated for the 3-tuple return (no other change).
